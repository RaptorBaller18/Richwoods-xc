How this ranking works

  • All-time rank compares the entered time with every individual record in the workbook. Race rank compares it only with records whose Race field matches your selection.
  • Each row is a recorded performance, not a unique athlete. If a runner appears in multiple years or races, each listed performance counts separately.
  • “Faster performances” counts records strictly faster than the entered time. A tie receives the same competition rank. Rankings reflect this workbook, not every race Richwoods has ever run.
